# Shipping and Delivery

## Where we deliver

We deliver everywhere inside the Kathmandu Valley with our own riders and ship to all major cities in Nepal, including Pokhara, Chitwan, Biratnagar and Butwal, through a partner courier.

## Delivery times

Inside the Kathmandu Valley, orders usually arrive within one to two working days after they are confirmed. Other cities usually take three to five working days. We do not deliver on Saturdays.

## Delivery charges

Delivery charges depend on the destination and are shown on your order summary before you confirm. The order summary always shows the final amount you will pay.

## Packaging

Spices are packed in sealed, food-grade pouches inside a cardboard box, so they keep their aroma in transit.
