# About Kathmandu Spice Co.

## Who we are

Kathmandu Spice Co. is a small spice merchant in Asan, the old market quarter of Kathmandu. We grind and blend in small batches and sell Nepali spices, masalas and pickling blends online to home cooks across Nepal.

## Where our spices come from

Timur and jimbu come from growers in the high hills of western and northern Nepal. Turmeric and cardamom come from farms in the eastern hills. We buy directly from farmer groups and visit them every season.

## How we make our blends

Every masala is roasted and ground in our own workshop in batches small enough to sell within a few weeks, so the spices stay aromatic. We use no fillers, colours or added salt in our masalas.
