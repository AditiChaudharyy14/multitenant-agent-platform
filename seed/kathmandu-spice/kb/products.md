# Product Catalogue

Descriptions only. Prices and availability always come from our live catalogue, never from this page.

## Kathmandu Garam Masala

SKU: KS-MSL-001
Category: masala
Description: our house garam masala of roasted cumin, coriander, cinnamon, cloves and black cardamom. Warm, not hot.
Use: stir into curries and dal at the end of cooking.

## Momo Masala

SKU: KS-MSL-002
Category: masala
Description: a savoury blend of timur, coriander, cumin and dried chilli for momo fillings and dipping achar.
Use: mix into minced filling or roast into tomato achar.

## Newari Choila Spice Mix

SKU: KS-MSL-003
Category: masala
Description: a smoky, hot mix of chilli, timur, fenugreek and mustard for Newari choila.
Use: rub on grilled meat or mushrooms with mustard oil.

## Timur Sichuan Pepper

SKU: KS-WHL-001
Category: whole
Description: whole timur berries from western Nepal, with a citrusy aroma and a tingling, numbing heat.
Use: toast and crush for achar, chutneys and noodle soups.

## Jimbu Himalayan Herb

SKU: KS-WHL-002
Category: whole
Description: dried jimbu, a mountain allium with a mild onion-garlic flavour, traditionally fried in ghee for dal.
Use: temper in hot ghee or oil before adding to lentils.

## Large Black Cardamom

SKU: KS-WHL-003
Category: whole
Description: smoke-dried large cardamom pods from the eastern hills, with a deep, smoky aroma.
Use: add whole to rice, meat curries and masala tea.

## Turmeric Powder

SKU: KS-GRD-001
Category: ground
Description: bright, earthy turmeric from the eastern hills, ground in small batches.
Use: a pinch in dal, vegetables and pickles.

## Achar Pickling Blend

SKU: KS-PKL-001
Category: pickling
Description: mustard seed, fenugreek, nigella and chilli for radish, cucumber and mixed vegetable achar.
