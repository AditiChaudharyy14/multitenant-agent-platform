# Frequently Asked Questions

### Do you deliver outside Kathmandu Valley?

Yes. We deliver inside the Kathmandu Valley with our own riders and ship to all major cities in Nepal through a partner courier. Remote hill districts may take a few extra days.

### How long does delivery take?

Inside the Kathmandu Valley, delivery usually takes one to two working days after your order is confirmed. Other cities usually take three to five working days. We do not deliver on Saturdays or major public holidays.

### How can I pay for my order?

We accept cash on delivery only. You pay the rider when your parcel arrives.

### Can I change my order after placing it?

Yes, as long as the order is still pending. You can add or remove items, change quantities or update the delivery address. Once our team has confirmed the order it has already been packed and can no longer be changed.

### How do I cancel an order?

Ask our assistant to cancel it. Orders can be cancelled only while they are still pending; after that, you can refuse the parcel on delivery or use our returns process.

### How do I track my order?

Ask our assistant for the status of your order. Each order is pending, confirmed, shipped, delivered or cancelled, and the assistant can show when each stage happened.

### What happens if a tea is out of stock?

Our assistant checks live availability before any order is placed and will suggest a similar tea from the same category. We never take an order for a tea we cannot send.

### Are your teas organic?

Many of our partner gardens farm without synthetic pesticides, and several hold organic certification. Each product page names its origin; ask us if you need certification details for a specific tea.

### Where do your teas come from?

Almost all of our teas come from small gardens and cooperatives in the eastern hills of Nepal, mainly Ilam, Dhankuta and Taplejung. Our herbal infusions use herbs grown in the Terai and wild-harvested Himalayan nettle.

### How should I store loose-leaf tea?

Keep tea in its resealable pouch or an airtight tin, away from light, heat, moisture and strong smells such as spices or coffee. Never store tea in the fridge, where it picks up moisture.

### How long does tea stay fresh?

Green and white teas taste best within six months of opening. Black teas, oolongs and chai blends keep their flavour for about a year if they are stored well.

### Do your teas contain caffeine?

All true teas (green, black, oolong and white) contain caffeine. Black tea and chai blends contain the most, and white and green teas generally contain a little less.

### Which teas are caffeine-free?

Our herbal infusions are naturally caffeine-free: Tulsi Lemongrass Infusion, Himalayan Nettle and Mint, and Chamomile Calm.

### Can I return an opened pack of tea?

We cannot accept returns of opened tea for food-safety reasons unless the tea is faulty. Unopened pouches can be returned within fourteen days of delivery. If an opened tea disappoints you, contact us and we will usually offer a replacement.

### Do you offer gift wrapping?

Yes. Ask for gift wrapping when you place your order and we will wrap it in handmade lokta paper with a handwritten card at no extra charge.

### Do you sell wholesale or to cafés?

We supply a small number of cafés and hotels in the Kathmandu Valley. Please message our team with your business name and the teas you are interested in, and we will get back to you within two working days.

### Can I reorder a previous order?

Yes. Ask our assistant to reorder any of your past orders. It will rebuild the order using today's prices and availability and show you a summary to confirm before anything is placed.

### Do you have a loyalty or discount programme?

We occasionally run seasonal promotions, which are announced on our website and applied automatically at checkout. We do not offer discount codes through chat.

### Can I visit your tasting room?

Yes. Our tasting room in Jhamsikhel, Lalitpur, is open to visitors on Sunday to Friday afternoons. You can taste any tea from our range before you buy.

### How do I brew masala chai at home?

Simmer water with our Kathmandu Masala Chai for two minutes, add milk and sugar, bring it back to a gentle boil and simmer for two more minutes before straining. Our Brewing Guides have exact quantities.

### Can I order teaware on its own?

Yes. Our Clay Kulhad Cup Set and Glass Teapot with Infuser can be ordered on their own or together with tea.
