# Returns and Refunds

## Our freshness promise

If a tea arrives stale, damaged or not as described, tell us within seven days of delivery and we will replace it or refund it, whichever you prefer.

## Unopened items

Unopened tea pouches and unused teaware can be returned within fourteen days of delivery for any reason. The item must be in its original packaging with the seal intact.

## Opened tea

For hygiene and food-safety reasons we cannot accept returns of opened tea pouches unless the tea is faulty. If an opened tea tastes wrong to you, contact us anyway: we would rather know, and we will usually offer a replacement of a different tea.

## Damaged teaware

If teaware arrives broken, please take a photo of the item and the box before throwing anything away and send it to us within forty-eight hours of delivery. We will send a replacement at no cost.

## How refunds are paid

Because we only accept cash on delivery, refunds are paid by bank transfer or mobile wallet (eSewa or Khalti) to an account in the customer's name. Refunds are processed within five working days after we receive the returned item.

## How to start a return

Ask our assistant or message our team with your order number and the items you want to return. We will arrange a rider to collect the item inside the Kathmandu Valley, or give you courier instructions for other cities.
