# About Himali Tea House

## Who we are

Himali Tea House is a small, family-run tea merchant based in Lalitpur, in the Kathmandu Valley. We started as a tea stall near Patan Durbar Square and now sell loose-leaf teas, chai blends and teaware online to customers across Nepal.

## Where our tea comes from

Most of our teas come from small growers in the eastern hills of Nepal, especially Ilam, Dhankuta and the slopes facing Kanchenjunga. We buy directly from cooperatives and family gardens, visit them every season, and pay above the commodity rate so growers can invest in hand-plucking and careful processing.

## What we sell

We sell green, black, oolong and white teas, caffeine-free herbal infusions, spiced chai blends, and a small range of teaware. Every tea is packed in resealable, light-proof pouches in our Lalitpur workshop.

## Our values

We believe good tea should be traceable. Every product page names the region it comes from, and we never blend in leaf from outside Nepal without saying so. We avoid single-use plastic in our shipping and reuse cardboard wherever we can.

## Contact and opening hours

Our team answers messages Sunday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 Nepal time. Our tasting room in Jhamsikhel is open to visitors Sunday to Friday afternoons. Online orders can be placed at any time.
