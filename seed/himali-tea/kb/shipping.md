# Shipping and Delivery

## Where we deliver

We deliver everywhere inside the Kathmandu Valley (Kathmandu, Lalitpur and Bhaktapur) with our own riders. Outside the valley we ship through a partner courier to all major cities, including Pokhara, Chitwan, Biratnagar, Butwal, Dharan and Nepalgunj. Remote hill districts may need a few extra days.

## Delivery times

Orders inside the Kathmandu Valley are usually delivered within one to two working days after they are confirmed. Orders to other cities usually take three to five working days. We do not deliver on Saturdays or on major public holidays such as Dashain and Tihar.

## Delivery charges

Delivery charges depend on the destination and are shown on your order summary before you confirm. Larger orders may qualify for free delivery; the order summary always shows the final amount you will pay.

## Payment

We currently accept cash on delivery only. Please keep the exact amount ready if you can; our riders carry limited change.

## Order status

Every order moves through these stages: pending (we have received it), confirmed (our team has checked and packed it), shipped (it is with a rider or courier), and delivered. You can ask our assistant for the current status of any of your orders at any time.

## Changing or cancelling an order

You can change or cancel an order while it is still pending. Once our team has confirmed it, the order has been packed and can no longer be edited or cancelled; you can instead refuse the parcel or use our returns process.

## Failed deliveries

If our rider cannot reach you, they will call twice and try again the next working day. After two failed attempts the parcel returns to our workshop and we will contact you to arrange a new delivery.
