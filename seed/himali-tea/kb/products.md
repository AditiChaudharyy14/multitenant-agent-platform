# Product Catalogue

Descriptions only. Prices and availability always come from our live catalogue, never from this page.

## Ilam First Flush Green

SKU: HT-GRN-001
Category: green
Origin: Ilam, eastern Nepal; spring (first flush) harvest.
Tasting notes: fresh and grassy with a light sweetness and a hint of toasted rice.
Brewing: 75–80°C for two to three minutes; re-steeps well.
Similar teas: Himalayan Sencha, Kanchenjunga Mist Green.

## Jasmine Pearl Green

SKU: HT-GRN-002
Category: green
Origin: green leaf from Dhankuta, hand-rolled into pearls and scented with fresh jasmine blossoms.
Tasting notes: floral and perfumed, smooth body, no bitterness when brewed cool.
Brewing: 75°C for three minutes; the pearls unfurl over several steeps.
Similar teas: Ilam First Flush Green.

## Himalayan Sencha

SKU: HT-GRN-003
Category: green
Origin: steamed green tea from high gardens in Ilam, made in the Japanese style.
Tasting notes: bright, vegetal and slightly marine with a clean finish.
Brewing: 70–75°C for two minutes.
Similar teas: Kanchenjunga Mist Green, Ilam First Flush Green.

## Kanchenjunga Mist Green

SKU: HT-GRN-004
Category: green
Origin: a rare, small-batch green tea from a single garden facing Kanchenjunga, often wrapped in morning mist.
Tasting notes: sweet chestnut, orchid and a long, buttery finish.
Brewing: 75°C for two to three minutes.
Similar teas: Himalayan Sencha is the closest everyday alternative when this small batch runs out.

## Ilam Second Flush Black

SKU: HT-BLK-001
Category: black
Origin: Ilam, summer (second flush) harvest.
Tasting notes: muscatel grape, malt and a gentle astringency.
Brewing: 95°C for three to four minutes; lovely plain or with a splash of milk.
Similar teas: Golden Tips Black, Smoky Lapsang Hills.

## Golden Tips Black

SKU: HT-BLK-002
Category: black
Origin: hand-picked golden buds from a cooperative garden in Ilam.
Tasting notes: honey, cocoa and sweet potato, very smooth.
Brewing: 90–95°C for four minutes.
Similar teas: Ilam Second Flush Black.

## Everest Breakfast Black

SKU: HT-BLK-003
Category: black
Origin: a robust blend of Nepali black teas from Ilam and Dhankuta.
Tasting notes: strong and malty; made to take milk and sugar.
Brewing: boiling water for four to five minutes.
Similar teas: Ilam Second Flush Black.

## Smoky Lapsang Hills

SKU: HT-BLK-004
Category: black
Origin: black tea from Dhankuta, slowly dried over pine wood fires.
Tasting notes: campfire smoke, pine resin and dried longan.
Brewing: 95°C for three to four minutes.
Similar teas: Ilam Second Flush Black is the closest alternative when this tea is unavailable.

## Himalayan Amber Oolong

SKU: HT-OOL-001

Origin: a medium-oxidised rolled oolong from Ilam.

## Rhododendron Valley Oolong

Origin: a tiny seasonal lot from a high garden surrounded by wild rhododendron forest.
