# About Lotus Wellness Studio

## Who we are

Lotus Wellness Studio is a calm, light-filled studio in Sanepa, Lalitpur, offering therapeutic massage, physiotherapy and private yoga. We opened to bring together hands-on bodywork and movement therapy under one roof, so clients can recover, relax and build strength in the same place.

## Our approach

Every first visit starts with a short conversation about your health, goals and any injuries. Our practitioners adapt each session to you, and we coordinate between massage, physiotherapy and yoga when a client sees more than one of us.

## The studio

We have three private treatment rooms, a quiet yoga room with natural light, changing rooms with lockers, and a tea corner where you can rest after your session. The studio is on the ground floor and is wheelchair accessible.

## Opening days

We are open Sunday to Friday and closed on Saturdays. Friday hours are shorter than the rest of the week, and we close on some public holidays. Our assistant always checks the live schedule when you ask about a specific day or time.

## Location and parking

We are on Sanepa Main Road, a short walk from Sanepa Chowk. There is free parking for two-wheelers in front of the studio and limited car parking in the lane behind it.
