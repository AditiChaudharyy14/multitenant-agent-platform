# Our Practitioners

## Sunita Rai

Sunita is our lead massage therapist. She trained in Swedish and deep tissue massage in Kathmandu and in hot stone therapy in Chiang Mai, and has more than ten years of experience. Clients describe her as calm, attentive and very good at finding knots. She offers Swedish Relaxation Massage, Deep Tissue Massage and Hot Stone Therapy.

## Dr. Prakash Thapa

Dr. Prakash is a physiotherapist with a doctorate in physical therapy and a background in sports medicine. He worked with national football and athletics squads before joining Lotus. He offers Physiotherapy Assessment, Sports Injury Rehab and Deep Tissue Massage, and he is the right person for injuries, post-surgery recovery and persistent pain.

## Maya Tamang

Maya is a certified yoga teacher with advanced training in prenatal yoga and a decade of teaching in Pokhara and Kathmandu. She also trained in Swedish massage. She offers Private Hatha Yoga, Prenatal Yoga and Swedish Relaxation Massage, and she is known for making beginners feel at ease.
