# Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to book in advance?

Yes, all sessions are by appointment. Evenings and Sundays fill up fastest, so booking a few days ahead gives you the most choice.

### How do I book an appointment?

Tell our assistant which treatment you want and when you would like to come. It will check live availability, suggest times, and show you a summary to confirm before anything is booked.

### Can I reschedule my appointment?

Yes. Ask our assistant to move your appointment. Rescheduling is free more than 24 hours before the start time; within 24 hours it is treated like a late cancellation.

### What is your cancellation policy?

Cancelling more than 24 hours ahead is free. Cancelling within 24 hours of the start time costs 50% of the service price.

### What happens if I arrive late?

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your session may be shortened so the next client is not delayed, and the full price still applies. Please let us know if you are running late.

### What should I wear for a massage?

Wear whatever is comfortable. You undress to your comfort level in a private room and are covered with a sheet throughout, with only the area being worked on uncovered.

### What should I bring to a yoga session?

Just comfortable clothes you can move in and a bottle of water. Mats, blocks, straps and bolsters are provided. Try not to eat a heavy meal in the two hours before.

### Do you offer prenatal sessions?

Yes. Maya Tamang teaches one-to-one Prenatal Yoga, suitable from the second trimester with your doctor's approval. Hot Stone Therapy is not offered during pregnancy.

### Is physiotherapy suitable after surgery?

Often, yes. Dr. Prakash Thapa works with post-surgery recovery. Please bring your discharge notes and any instructions from your surgeon to your Physiotherapy Assessment.

### Do I need a doctor's referral for physiotherapy?

No referral is needed. If you have reports, scans or a referral letter, please bring them to your first appointment.

### Can I choose my practitioner?

Yes. Tell our assistant who you would like to see. If you have no preference, it will offer the earliest time with any practitioner who provides that service.

### Do you have female practitioners?

Yes. Sunita Rai (massage) and Maya Tamang (yoga and Swedish massage) are both women. You can ask for either when booking.

### Are you open on Saturdays?

No, the studio is closed every Saturday. We are open Sunday to Friday, with shorter hours on Fridays.

### What are your opening hours?

We are open Sunday to Friday and closed on Saturdays; Fridays close earlier than other days, and we close on some public holidays. Ask our assistant for a specific day and it will check the live schedule.

### How do I pay?

You pay at the studio after your session, by cash, card or mobile wallet. Nothing is charged when you book online.

### Is there parking?

There is free two-wheeler parking in front of the studio and limited car parking in the lane behind it.

### Can I book for someone else?

Each booking belongs to the account that made it, so the person attending should create their own account and book. This keeps their health information private.

### What if I feel unwell before my appointment?

Please stay home if you have a fever or anything contagious, and tell us as early as possible. Our usual cancellation policy applies, but we waive the late fee for genuine emergencies.

### Do you offer gift vouchers?

Gift vouchers can be bought at the studio reception for any of our services. They are valid for six months.

### Is there a minimum age?

Massage and yoga clients must be at least sixteen; under-eighteens need a parent or guardian present for their first visit. Physiotherapy is available for younger patients with a parent present.
