# Our Services

Descriptions only. Prices, durations offered and availability come from our live booking system.

## Swedish Relaxation Massage

A full-body massage with long, flowing strokes and light to medium pressure, using warm sesame or almond oil. Ideal for stress relief, better sleep and first-time massage clients.
Offered by: Sunita Rai, Maya Tamang.

## Deep Tissue Massage

Slow, firm pressure that works into deeper layers of muscle and fascia. Good for chronic tension in the neck, shoulders and lower back, and for people who train hard. Some soreness the next day is normal.
Offered by: Sunita Rai, Dr. Prakash Thapa.

## Hot Stone Therapy

Smooth, heated basalt stones are placed on key points and used as an extension of the therapist's hands. The warmth relaxes muscles quickly and makes this a deeply calming treatment. Not suitable during pregnancy.
Offered by: Sunita Rai.

## Physiotherapy Assessment

A first appointment with our physiotherapist: a history of your problem, movement and strength tests, a clear explanation of what is going on, and a home exercise plan. Recommended before starting Sports Injury Rehab.
Offered by: Dr. Prakash Thapa.

## Sports Injury Rehab

Follow-up physiotherapy sessions for sprains, strains, tendon problems and post-surgery recovery, combining manual therapy, guided exercise and taping where useful.
Offered by: Dr. Prakash Thapa.

## Private Hatha Yoga

A one-to-one hatha yoga session tailored to your level, from complete beginners to experienced students. Sessions combine postures, breathing (pranayama) and relaxation. Mats and props are provided.
Offered by: Maya Tamang.

## Prenatal Yoga

Gentle, one-to-one yoga for pregnancy, focusing on breathing, pelvic floor awareness, back comfort and preparation for birth. Suitable from the second trimester with your doctor's approval.
Offered by: Maya Tamang.
