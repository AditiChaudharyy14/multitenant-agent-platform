# Cancellation and Late Arrival Policy

## Cancelling more than 24 hours ahead

You can cancel any appointment free of charge if you do so more than 24 hours before it is due to start. Ask our assistant or call the studio.

## Cancelling within 24 hours

If you cancel within 24 hours of the start time, a cancellation fee of 50% of the service price applies. This lets us pay our practitioners for time they reserved for you. The fee is recorded on your booking and settled at your next visit.

## Rescheduling

Moving an appointment to a different time is free when you do it more than 24 hours ahead. A reschedule within 24 hours of the original start time is treated like a cancellation and the 50% fee applies.

## Arriving late

Please arrive five to ten minutes early. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, your session may be shortened so the next client is not kept waiting, and the full price still applies. If you are running late, let us know as soon as you can.

## Missed appointments

If you miss an appointment without telling us, it is treated as a late cancellation and the 50% fee applies.

## Illness and emergencies

If you are unwell, especially with a fever or anything contagious, please do not come in. Tell us as early as you can. We waive the late cancellation fee for genuine emergencies at the studio manager's discretion.

## When we cancel

If we ever have to cancel because a practitioner is ill, we will contact you straight away, offer the nearest available alternative times, and never charge a fee.
